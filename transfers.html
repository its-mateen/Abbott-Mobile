How a transfer works
Stock only moves when a unit is shipped and again when it is received
1 · Request

The receiving branch lists the products it needs. Nothing leaves the source yet.

2 · Approve

The source branch manager (or a Super Admin) approves or rejects with a reason.

3 · Ship

Units are picked by IMEI / serial, leave the source stock and are marked in transit.

4 · Inspect & receive

Each unit is scanned and marked OK, missing or damaged before it becomes sellable stock.
