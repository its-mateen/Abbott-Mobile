The estimated bank position is negative
Bank and wallet payments out (supplier payments and expenses) exceed non-cash money collected, so the estimate is . A negative balance is not an asset, so it is shown under liabilities as Bank overdraft / owner funding: that gap is owner capital and opening bank balances, which this prototype does not record yet — the value of the stock those payments bought is already sitting in Inventory at cost.
How these numbers are built
Cash in drawer — the last closed day-end actual cash for each branch on or before the date, plus cash taken in since (sales, khata receipts, repair payments) minus cash paid out (expenses, refunds, supplier payments).
Bank & wallets (estimated) — every non-cash collection (card, JazzCash, EasyPaisa, bank transfer) less non-cash expenses and supplier payments. It is an estimate, not a reconciled bank balance; when the net is negative it moves to the liability side as bank overdraft / owner funding.
Receivables aging — a customer khata is counted once, against the branch of its latest entry, so the branch columns add up to the all-branch total.
Inventory at cost — current branch stock valued at weighted average cost. Stock is not rewound to a past date, so historical views still show today's inventory.
Receivables & payables — customer and supplier ledgers replayed up to the date; each balance is shown against the branch of its latest transaction.
Repair dues — the unpaid balance on every repair job that has not been cancelled.
Repair advances held — money already collected on jobs that have not yet been delivered; it is still the customer's until the device is handed over.
