Inventory
Stock alerts and validation rules used by POS, purchases, repairs and the unit registry.
Low-stock alerts
Show products at or below their minimum stock on the dashboard and in the notification bell.
IMEI check digit (Luhn)
Reject IMEIs whose 15th digit fails the Luhn checksum when registering phones, tablets and watches. Turn off if a supplier ships non-standard IMEIs.
Upper-case serial numbers
Store laptop / desktop serial numbers in capitals (5cg1234abc → 5CG1234ABC) so scans and typed lookups always match. Lookups are case-insensitive either way.
Allow negative stock
Let cashiers sell non-serialized items that show zero quantity. Stock goes negative until the purchase is entered. Not recommended — it distorts stock valuation and profit reports.
IMEI vs. serial number — how units are tracked
IMEIPhones, tablets, smartwatches

Exactly 15 digits, optionally validated with the Luhn check digit above. Dual-SIM phones can store a second IMEI. Example: 356789012345678

Serial No.Laptops, desktops, all-in-ones

Manufacturer serial of 4–40 letters, digits or - _ . /. Example: 5CG1234ABC, FVFM1-2345

The product's category decides which applies. Both live in the same unit registry (IMEI Registry), must be unique, and are matched case-insensitively at POS, in transfers and at repair intake. Per-product minimum stock is set on each product; serialized products never go negative — every unit must be registered before it can be sold.
